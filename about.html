About Smart Traffic Nashik

A simple digital platform designed to provide useful traffic information and make travelling across Nashik easier and safer.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making Traffic Information Simple & Accessible

Smart Traffic Nashik is a web-based traffic management project created to provide users with useful information related to traffic conditions, routes, alerts and emergency services in Nashik.

The website brings important traffic-related facilities together in one place so that users can access information quickly while travelling.

The project focuses on creating a simple, user-friendly and informative platform for everyday commuters.

OUR OBJECTIVE

What We Want To Achieve

Easy Traffic Information

Provide traffic-related information in a simple and understandable format.

Better Route Planning

Help users explore route options for travelling across Nashik.

Traffic Awareness

Display traffic alerts and important road-related information.

Quick Emergency Access

Make important emergency contact information easier to access.

KEY FEATURES

Everything In One Place

Live Traffic Map

View traffic conditions and traffic signal information.

Route Suggestion

Explore routes between different locations in Nashik.

Traffic Alerts

Stay informed about important traffic-related alerts.

Complaint Facility

Report traffic problems such as accidents, jams and roadblocks.

Quickly find important emergency contact information.

Check simulated traffic signal status and countdown information.
